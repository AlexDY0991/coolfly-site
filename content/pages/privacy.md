This policy explains what personal data CoolFly collects through this website and how we use it.

## Who we are

CoolFly is a personal aircraft company based in Gongshu District, Hangzhou, Zhejiang, China. For any privacy question, email [support@coolflyaircraft.com](mailto:support@coolflyaircraft.com).

## What we collect

- **Details you send us.** When you fill in a form (for example a dealer application or a demo request), we collect the information you enter, such as your name, company, email address, phone number, country and message.
- **How you found us.** With your form we record the page you sent it from and, where present, campaign information in the link you followed (such as `utm_source` or an advertising click ID).
- **Cookies and similar technologies.** With your consent, we use analytics and advertising cookies to measure visits and the performance of our advertising. You can change your choice at any time with the "Cookie settings" link at the bottom of every page.

## How we use it

We use your details to reply to your enquiry, to assess dealer applications, and to understand which pages and campaigns bring enquiries. We do not sell your personal data.

## Who we share it with

We use service providers to run this website, receive form submissions and measure traffic. They process data on our behalf and only for these purposes.

## How long we keep it

We keep enquiry data for as long as needed to respond and to manage any resulting business relationship, and then delete it.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, or to object to its use. To make a request, email [support@coolflyaircraft.com](mailto:support@coolflyaircraft.com).
