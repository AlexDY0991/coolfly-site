CoolFly is leading a new way to travel through the sky at low altitudes. If you own a standing aircraft like the CoolFly Urban-2, you might wonder where you can fly it. This guide helps you find the right spots. We will look at rules for a VTOL aircraft and how to stay safe while having fun.

## **What is a VTOL aircraft and why does it matter?**

Many people ask, “[what is VTOL aircraft?](https://coolflyaircraft.com/what-is-evtol-aircraft-stunning-coolfly-secrets/)” The term stands for Vertical Take-Off and Landing. These machines do not need a long runway. They go straight up and down like a helicopter. However, electric VTOL aircraft are much quieter. They use batteries instead of loud engines.

The future of travel is moving to the “low-altitude economy.” Experts think this market will be worth trillions of dollars soon. CoolFly is a big part of this shift. They focus on the space between 0 and 30 meters above the ground. This area is less crowded than the high sky where big planes fly. By staying low, CoolFly helps you avoid complex flight paths.

## **Understanding the legal path: FAA Part 103**

In the United States, the government has a special rule for small flyers. It is called FAA Part 103. This is the “gold standard” for ultralight aircraft. [If a craft follows these rules, you do not need a pilot’s license. You also do not need to register the craft with the government.](http://aerospaceglobalnews.com)

To fit this rule, the craft must be for one person only. It must weigh less than 254 pounds (about 115.2 kg) if it is powered by a motor.Also has a speed limit. It cannot go faster than 55 knots (about 102 km/h). CoolFly designs its products to meet these types of global standards. This means more freedom for you. In Europe, countries like Italy and France also have flexible rules for “recreational” eVTOL flights.

## **Choosing your Coolfly flight location**

![coolfly eVTOL flying in the city](/assets/img/news/coolfly-best-spots-for-your-future-evtol-aircraft-1.jpg)

Finding a place to fly requires some planning. You cannot just take off from a busy city street. The CoolFly manual suggests a few key things to look for in a site.

1\. Suburban and open areas

The best place is away from the city center. Look for open fields or resorts. You need a flat space with a radius of at least 20 meters. This gives you about 1,256 square meters of room. This space is enough to land safely and keep people at a distance.

2\. Private camps and parks

Many people use their electric VTOL aircraft at private flying camps. Places like the Jiulong Lake Resort in Guangzhou are great examples. These spots often have charging piles and staff to help you. You can fly over the water or near green hills. It is a great way to see nature from a new view.

3\. High-rise rescue zones

CoolFly is not just for fun. It is also for work. In the future, these machines will help firefighters. They can fly up to a burning building much faster than a ladder truck. If a building is 10 floors high, a standing aircraft can reach the window in seconds. This makes urban safety much better.

## **Top International Flight Spots for Your Coolfly**

Many regions are now opening their skies. Based on industry trends and government reports, here are the best places to fly.

1\. Tuscany, Italy: The Green Sandbox

Italy is a leader in recreational eVTOL flight. The Italian Civil Aviation Authority (ENAC) is very flexible. Reports show that companies like Jetson have already moved their testing to Tuscany.

The rolling hills and open vineyards are perfect for a CoolFly Urban-2. The wind is often light, and the views are amazing. Tuscany has many private estates and “agriturismo” farms. These spots offer flat, private land for a 20-meter radius takeoff zone. Flying over Italian olive groves at 10 meters high is a dream come true.

2\. The American Southwest: Nevada and Arizona

The United States has a very strong “ultralight” culture. The deserts of Nevada and Arizona offer thousands of acres of open space. Under FAA Part 103, you can fly in “uncontrolled” airspace.

These desert areas are ideal because they are far from busy airports. You can find dry lake beds that are perfectly flat. This is the best place to test the 70 km/h speed of a vtol aircraft. The clear weather also means you have great visibility. Just remember to stay away from the Las Vegas “red zones” or military bases.

3\. NEOM and the Red Sea Project, Saudi Arabia

Saudi Arabia is building the future of cities. Their NEOM project is a “living lab” for electric vtol aircraft. The government wants to use these machines for daily travel.

Reports state that Saudi Arabia has created “regulatory sandboxes.” This means they allow new types of flight that other countries might still be studying. The Red Sea Project has many luxury resorts on islands. Flying a CoolFly between a beach and a villa is exactly what these places are designed for. The air is warm, and the infrastructure is being built for charging piles right now.

4\. The French Countryside: ULM Class 6

France has a very mature system for small aircraft. They have a category called “ULM Class 6” specifically for ultralight helicopters and multicopters. This makes the French countryside a very friendly place for CoolFly.

Places like the Loire Valley or the fields of Provence are excellent. There are many small, grass airfields across France. These sites often have clubs where you can meet other flyers. The French rules allow you to fly in many rural areas as long as you stay away from crowds. It is a very peaceful way to see historic chateaus from above.

5\. Dubai and Abu Dhabi, UAE

The UAE wants to be the world’s eVTOL hub. They have already set standards for “vertiports” or landing pads. While many projects focus on “air taxis,” they are also very open to personal flight clusters.

The “Advanced Air Mobility” (AAM) centers in Abu Dhabi are great for tech lovers. These are controlled zones where you can fly safely with the latest 5G support. You can enjoy a futuristic flight with a view of some of the world’s most modern buildings.

## **Steps to get permission** **of Coolfly**

Even if you do not need a pilot’s license, you still need to tell the local authorities. Most places require you to follow a simple process.

**Submit an application:** You should talk to the local police or air traffic control. Do this 3 to 5 days before you plan to fly.

Share your plan: Tell them where you will fly and for how long. They will check if the area is safe.

**Check “No-Fly Zones”:** Never fly near airports or military bases. These are “red zones.” If you fly there, you can get into big trouble.

**Watch the weather:** The Dream-2 model can handle wind up to level 7. But if there is a big storm, it is best to stay on the ground.

## **Coolfly** **Technical limits to remember**

Every vtol aircraft has limits. The CoolFly Urban-2 has a software limit for height. It stays below 10 meters for safety. It can fly for 15 to 20 minutes on one charge. The Dream-2 can go higher, up to 450 meters. It can also fly for up to 30 minutes.

When you choose a spot, make sure you have power. A 40kWh power supply is recommended for charging. Since CoolFly uses standard electric car plugs, you can often use a regular car charging station. This makes it very easy to “refuel” while you are on a trip.

## How to Choose Your Own Spot

If you are not near these famous spots, you can still find a great place. Use these facts from the CoolFly manual to judge a site:

**Look for the “Suburbs”:** Stay away from city centers. The best spots are in the “fringe” areas where there are fewer people.

**Check for Power:** CoolFly uses car-style charging. Look for resorts or parks that already have electric vehicle (EV) charging stations.

**Verify Airspace:** Use a flight map app to check for “Class G” airspace. This is usually the safest place for a vtol aircraft.

**Flat Surface:** You need a space at least 20 meters wide. A mowed field, a private driveway, or a resort courtyard works well.

## **The global trend**

The low-altitude world is growing fast. In China, 31 provinces have put this into their government reports. They want to build more “flying parks.” This will make finding a legal spot even easier in the next few years. We are seeing a move from “special permits” to “standard flying.”

By 2030, thousands of these craft will be in the sky. CoolFly wants to make sure everyone can fly safely. Whether you are at a beach in Italy or a field in China, the rules are becoming clearer. The goal is to let everyone “define their own altitude.”

CoolFly makes flight safe with “six-redundancy” systems. This means even if one part fails, the machine stays stable. However, a good pilot always checks the ground. Avoid power lines, tall trees, and crowded beaches.

In the next few years, we will see more “flying camps.” These will be like golf courses but for electric vtol aircraft. They will have staff, charging, and clear paths. This will make the hobby even easier for everyone.

## **In the Future**

Flying a CoolFly aircraft is an amazing experience. To do it right, choose open spaces in the suburbs. Stay away from restricted zones. Always talk to local authorities a few days early.

As VTOL aircraft technology gets better, our world will feel much smaller. You are not just flying a machine; you are joining the future of travel.
