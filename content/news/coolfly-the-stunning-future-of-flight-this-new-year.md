The Chinese New Year is the most important time in China. It is a season for family. It is also a time to look at the future. For a company like Coolfly, this holiday has a special meaning. We see millions of people traveling to reach their homes. This massive movement of people makes us think about the sky.

![](/assets/img/news/coolfly-the-stunning-future-of-flight-this-new-year-1.webp)

Many people ask, [what is vtol aircraft?](https://coolflyaircraft.com/what-is-evtol-aircraft-stunning-coolfly-secrets/) The answer is quite simple. These are vehicles that can take off and land straight up and down. They do not need a long runway. Most of these machines are now electric. This is why we call them electric vtol aircraft or simply evtol. During the holiday rush, the roads on the ground are very crowded. Cars sit in long lines for hours. We believe we can solve this problem in the sky.

The Year of the Horse starts in 2026 in Chinese culture. This animal represents speed and spirit. In ancient times, people used horses to travel across the land. Today, we are building the “horses” of the sky. Our team at Coolfly wants to make flight as natural as riding a horse. This is our big goal for the new season.

## **Reflection on Our Journey**

The Chinese New Year is a time to remember the past. Our team does not just have new ideas. We have deep experience. Our R&D Director has spent more than 13 years working on flight control tech. This is a long time in the world of flying machines. We started by building systems for farming and moving goods. We worked on small drones that fly through forests.

![coolfly evtol](/assets/img/news/coolfly-the-stunning-future-of-flight-this-new-year-2.webp)

These 13 years taught us one big thing. Safety is everything. You cannot have a future in the air without trust. Because of this, we build our own systems from the bottom up. We do not just buy parts and put them together. We create the brain of the aircraft. This history is our foundation. It allows us to move into the vtol aircraft market with confidence.

I have observed that many companies talk about the low-altitude economy. However, not many have the years of testing that we do. We have tested our flight logic in 6 different mass-produced models already. This experience is what we bring into the new year.

## **Reunion and the Power of Technology**

The core of the holiday is reunion. People want to be with the ones they love. In the past, distance was a big wall. Today, we use technology to break that wall. Coolfly focuses on a technology called gravity-sensing flight control.

This system is very special. Most planes are hard to fly. You need to study for a long time. Our system is different. It uses the balance of your own body. If you want to move forward, you just lean forward. The machine follows your instinct. This makes the evtol feel like a part of you.

![spirit of the horse](/assets/img/news/coolfly-the-stunning-future-of-flight-this-new-year-4.jpg)

During the holiday, many people talk about the “spirit of the horse.” To us, this spirit means moving with grace and power. Our gravity-sensing tech allows for this. It is “bionic flight.” The aircraft “sees” what the pilot wants to do. This makes travel fast and easy. Imagine a world where you do not have to wait in traffic to see your parents. You just lift off from your yard and fly home. That is the world Coolfly is building.

## **Trends in the Low-Altitude Economy**

2026 is a very big year for the flight industry. [Global experts agree that the low-altitude economy is growing fast.](https://www.transportation.gov/sites/dot.gov/files/2025-12/AAM%20National%20Strategy%202025.pdf) More governments are making rules for electric vtol aircraft. They want to see these machines in our cities.

I have noticed a shift in the market recently. People no longer want just “toys.” They want real tools for travel and rescue. Coolfly is ready for this change. Our machines use on-board edge computing. This means the computer on the aircraft makes decisions in a millisecond. It does not wait for a signal from a far-away server. This is vital for safety in busy skies.

![More governments are making rules](/assets/img/news/coolfly-the-stunning-future-of-flight-this-new-year-5.jpg)

We also focus on obstacle avoidance. When thousands of vehicles are in the air, they must not hit each other. Our tech allows the aircraft to “sense” its surroundings. This is the only way to make the sky safe for everyone. As we celebrate the new year, we see these trends becoming reality. The dream of personal flight is no longer far away.

The spring brings new life. At Coolfly, we feel this energy. We are moving from testing to the real world. We want to show people that evtol is not just for experts. It is for the medic who needs to save a life. It is for the father who wants to get home for dinner.

Our commitment is to keep innovating. We will keep refining our gravity-sensing systems. We will keep making our vtol aircraft lighter and stronger. The goal is to make the sky as easy to use as the ground.

As the fireworks light up the sky this New Year, we see our path. We see a sky full of clean, quiet, and safe flying machines. We invite everyone to join us in this journey. The future of flight is here. It is electric, it is smart, and it is for you.
