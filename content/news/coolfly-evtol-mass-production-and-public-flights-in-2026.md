### **How do electric vtol aircraft work for you?**

You might wonder, “how do electric vtol aircraft work” if you are standing up. The CoolFly Urban uses a very special technology called gravity-sensing control. Actually, it feels like riding a flying balance scooter. You do not need a steering wheel or a complex cockpit. You simply lean your body to move. If you lean forward, the craft moves forward. If you lean back, it slows down or stops. This makes the learning process very fast for a beginner.

![a person standing on a CoolFly Urban-2, leaning forward slightly to initiate flight.](/assets/img/news/coolfly-evtol-mass-production-and-public-flights-in-2026-2.webp)

The machine is built with T800 carbon fiber. This material is very light but also very strong. The Urban has a smart “brain” called the NA80 flight controller. This computer checks your balance thousands of times every second. It uses six sensors to make sure the flight is always steady. Because it is an electric vtol aircraft, it uses batteries instead of gasoline. You can even charge it at a standard electric car station. This makes the craft clean, quiet, and easy to use in a city or a park.

One of the best things about an evtol aircraft like the CoolFly is the legal side. In many countries, these machines fall under “ultralight” rules, [such as FAA Part 103. ### **Safety is the top priority**

You might feel nervous about standing on a flying platform. However, CoolFly uses “Civil Aviation Level” safety standards for a consumer product. The craft has a “Triple Protection” system.

![A technical 3D visualization of the CoolFly safety system. A glowing blue LiDAR pulse scanning the environment around the aircraft. A ghosted transparent view shows the internal redundant flight computers and the ballistic parachute compartment.](/assets/img/news/coolfly-evtol-mass-production-and-public-flights-in-2026-5.webp)

The first layer is basic redundancy. This means the craft has two of every important part. If one flight computer fails, the second one takes over in milliseconds. The second layer is active protection. The craft uses laser radar and AI to see obstacles like trees or buildings. It will not let you fly into a wall. The third layer is passive safety. If the power fails completely, a ballistic parachute will shoot out automatically. The craft also has safety airbags on the bottom. These layers ensure that you can enjoy the evtol aircraft with peace of mind.
