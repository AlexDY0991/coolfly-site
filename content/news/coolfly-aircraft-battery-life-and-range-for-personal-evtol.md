CoolFly is changing how we think about flying with its new personal aircraft. Most people want to know one thing before they try a vtol aircraft. They ask how long the battery lasts and how far the craft can go. This is a very important question. Battery technology is the heart of the future of flight. If the battery is strong, you can fly longer. If it is weak, your trip ends quickly. Today, we will look at the facts about battery life and range for these machines.

![a CoolFly Urban-2 standing eVTOL aircraft flying 10 meters above a futuristic green city park](/assets/img/news/coolfly-aircraft-battery-life-and-range-for-personal-evtol-1.webp)

## **How do electric vtol aircraft work with batteries?**

Before we talk about minutes and kilometers, we should answer a basic question. [How do electric vtol aircraft work?](https://coolflyaircraft.com/actually-coolfly-safe-the-brilliant-secret-of-unique-propfans/) These machines use many electric motors. The CoolFly Dream-2, for example, uses eight propellers. These motors need a lot of power to lift a person straight up. This is why the battery is so important.

![CoolFly Dream-2 aircraft showing the 8-rotor propulsion system](/assets/img/news/coolfly-aircraft-battery-life-and-range-for-personal-evtol-2.webp)

When you take off, the motors use the most energy. It is like a car driving up a very steep hill. Once you are in the air and moving forward, the energy use goes down a little bit. The battery must be strong enough to handle both the takeoff and the landing. CoolFly uses what they call “disruptive battery rigidity technology.” This helps the battery stay safe and strong even when the motors pull a lot of power.

## **Understanding battery life for CoolFly Urban-2**

The CoolFly Urban-2 is a standing aircraft. It is designed for short trips and fun. According to the technical data, the Urban-2 has a flight time of 15 to 20 minutes. This might sound short to some people. But you have to remember that this craft is for “low-altitude” flying.

![A CoolFly Urban-2 personal aircraft flying fast over a calm blue lake at 70km/h.](/assets/img/news/coolfly-aircraft-battery-life-and-range-for-personal-evtol-3.webp)

Most users stay in the 0-30 meter range. This is the “golden vacuum layer” where there is not much traffic. In 20 minutes, you can cover a lot of ground because the craft is fast. The Urban-2 can fly at 70 kilometers per hour. If you fly for 20 minutes at that speed, you can travel about 23 kilometers. That is a long distance for a personal trip across a park or a lake.

## **Why the CoolFly Dream-2 offers more range**

If you need to stay in the air longer, the CoolFly Dream-2 is the better choice. This model is a sitting aircraft. It has a larger frame and can carry more battery weight. The Dream-2 has a flight time of 15 to 30 minutes.

![The CoolFly Dream-2 sitting-style eVTOL aircraft cruising at 450 meters altitude over a scenic countryside landscape.](/assets/img/news/coolfly-aircraft-battery-life-and-range-for-personal-evtol-4.webp)

That extra 10 minutes makes a big difference. At a speed of 70 kilometers per hour, 30 minutes of flight gives you a range of 35 kilometers. This range is perfect for travel between different parts of a large city or a resort. The Dream-2 also has a “service ceiling” of 450 meters. Even though it can go high, the battery lasts longest when you stay at a steady speed and height.

## **What is vtol aircraft charging like?**

You do not want to wait all day to charge your electric vtol aircraft. CoolFly solved this problem in a smart way. They use the same charging ports that electric cars use. This means you can go to a standard car charging station and plug in your aircraft.

![A CoolFly Urban-2 parked at a modern electric vehicle (EV) charging station. The pilot is plugging a standard charging cable into the aircraft's port.](/assets/img/news/coolfly-aircraft-battery-life-and-range-for-personal-evtol-5.webp)

Actually, Coolfly suggests using a 40kWh power supply for the best results. Because the craft uses standard car technology, the charging is fast and easy to find. You do not need a special airport to “refuel.” You can charge it at your house or at a camp site. This makes the vtol aircraft much more useful for daily life.

## **Factors that change your flight range**

Range is not just a single number. Many things can change how far your eVTOL goes. For example, weight is very important. The Urban-2 can carry 110kg. If a person weighs 100kg, the battery will work harder. If the person weighs 70kg, the battery will last longer.

![ First-person pilot view (POV) of the CoolFly NA80 flight controller dashboard.](/assets/img/news/coolfly-aircraft-battery-life-and-range-for-personal-evtol-6.webp)

Wind is another factor. The Dream-2 can handle level 7 winds. But flying against a strong wind uses more battery. It is like swimming against a river current. You have to use more energy to move forward. Also, if you fly very high, you use more power. CoolFly uses the NA80 flight controller to help manage this. It calculates the energy left and tells you exactly when you need to land. This helps you stay safe and never run out of power in the air.

## **The future of battery technology**

[The market for these machines is growing fast. Experts think the low-altitude economy will reach 1.5 trillion RMB by 2026.](http://aerospaceglobalnews.com) As more people buy a vtol aircraft, battery technology will get even better.

![Macro shot of a futuristic solid-state battery cell for eVTOL aircraft.](/assets/img/news/coolfly-aircraft-battery-life-and-range-for-personal-evtol-7.webp)

Right now, we use high-quality lithium batteries. In the future, batteries will be lighter and hold more power. This will allow personal aircraft to fly for an hour or more. But even with today’s 30-minute limit, these machines are changing how we move. They are faster than cars in traffic and much more fun.

## **How to get the most from your CoolFly battery**

To keep your battery healthy, you should follow a few simple steps. First, do not let the battery go to zero. The flight controller will warn you when power is low. You should land as soon as you see the warning. Second, try to charge the battery in a cool place. Extreme heat can hurt the battery over time.

![A professional owner performing maintenance on a CoolFly aircraft inside a modern, clean garage.](/assets/img/news/coolfly-aircraft-battery-life-and-range-for-personal-evtol-8.webp)

Also, keep your propellers clean. If the propellers have dirt on them, the motors have to work harder. This uses more battery power. By taking care of the craft, you ensure that your range stays as long as possible.

## **Final thoughts on range and battery**

CoolFly is making the dream of personal flight real for everyone. The Urban-2 and Dream-2 provide enough range for most personal needs. Whether it is a 23km trip or a 35km trip, you can reach your destination quickly.

Using standard car charging makes these machines very easy to own. You do not need to be a professional pilot to understand how they work. The smart systems inside handle the complex math for you. As we move into the future, the battery life will only get better. For now, 15 to 30 minutes of flight is a huge step forward for the world of electric vtol aircraft.
