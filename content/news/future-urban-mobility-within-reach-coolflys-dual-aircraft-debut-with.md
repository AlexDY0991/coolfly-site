### Future Urban Mobility Within Reach: CoolFly’s Dual Aircraft Debut with Advanced Flight Control Systems

As a representative of Hangzhou’s cutting-edge tech enterprises, **CoolFly Technology** recently unveiled an impressive milestone — the first public demonstration of **two manned eVTOL aircraft flying simultaneously**. This groundbreaking showcase not only highlights new possibilities for **future urban air mobility** but also earned high recognition from government leaders in Hangzhou and Gongshu District.

The visiting delegation was led by Dai Jianping, President of Hangzhou Promotion Association and former Standing Committee Member of the CPC Hangzhou Committee, along with Ao Yuxin, Deputy Secretary and District Mayor of Gongshu District, accompanied by dozens of officials. The event drew enthusiastic responses and strong support.

### Hundreds of Test Flights Powered by Reliable Flight Control Systems

CoolFly Technology is committed to a philosophy of **“rigor and safety”**. With **hundreds of successful flight tests**, combined with its proprietary **flight control system technology**, the company delivered a live demonstration where aircraft performed smooth take-offs, landings, and synchronized maneuvers. This performance showcased the seamless integration of **safety, intelligence, and advanced flight control systems**, leaving the audience with a vivid impression that **the future of flying cars and eVTOL aircraft is already within reach**.

### Interview Q&A

**Q1: What are the key features of CoolFly’s aircraft?**  
A: Our product has three highlights: **safe travel, intelligent flight, and convenient mobility**. The aircraft is not only a futuristic commuting tool but also designed with a **compact, foldable structure** — making it small enough to fit into a car trunk for **anytime, anywhere travel**.

**Q2: What safety measures are in place?**  
A: Safety always comes first. We use **biometric pilot monitoring, passive safety systems, and multiple protective layers**. Additionally, the aircraft integrates the **world’s first AI gravity-sensing system**, **collision-avoidance radar**, and a **stabilized propeller design**. All of this is reinforced by our robust **flight control system**, ensuring reliable and secure operations.

**Q3: Is there a special R&D background?**  
A: Yes. In collaboration with the Nanjing University of Aeronautics and Astronautics lab, we developed the world’s first **fan-prop hybrid aircraft**, capable of carrying over **150 kg payload**. It also features **electric shared-charging technology** and advanced **flight control systems**, combining safety, intelligence, and convenience.

**Q4: Why did you choose to base operations in Gongshu District, Hangzhou?**  
A: Gongshu District offers strong **talent policies and supportive business services**. From office setup to daily operations, we have experienced a strong sense of belonging. The efficient government support has given us great confidence in our growth.

### Looking Ahead

CoolFly Technology will continue to deepen cooperation with Gongshu District, pushing the boundaries of **urban air mobility** with more innovative technologies, advanced **flight control systems**, and practical eVTOL applications. We sincerely thank the leaders of Hangzhou and Gongshu District for their trust and support, and we remain dedicated to shaping the future of **safe, intelligent, and accessible flying mobility**.

**The future is here — flying is within reach!**
