### At CoolFly, every flight is more than just a test—it’s another milestone proving the maturity and reliability of our technology.

Our 389th flight took us further, higher, and at more challenging tilt angles than before. Each achievement reinforces the confidence we have in our core **NA80 Flight Control System**, designed to deliver stability and safety in every condition.

### Expanding Flight Capabilities

During this mission, we successfully extended our **flight distance, altitude, and inclination performance**. These advancements are not just numbers—they are proof that our technology can continuously adapt and evolve while maintaining maximum reliability.

### The Core of Our Confidence: NA80

At the heart of this progress lies the **NA80 triple-redundant flight control system**. Engineered for resilience and precision, it ensures seamless stability even in demanding maneuvers. This mature technology empowers us to test boldly while knowing safety is never compromised.

### A Safer, Smarter Way to Fly

Every successful flight adds another layer of trust—for our team, our partners, and the future of urban air mobility. With **389 flights completed**, our aircraft and systems are not only tested but also proven in real-world conditions.

At CoolFly, our philosophy is simple yet powerful:

**Fly Safe, Fly Smarter, Go Easy.**

And with every mission, we’re bringing that future closer.
