On August 10, 2026, CoolFly officially unveiled Urban, its standing-position personal aircraft, at the CoolFly Global Product Launch in Hangzhou, China.

![CoolFly CEO](/assets/img/news/coolfly-launches-urban-personal-aircraft-bringing-personal-flight-1.jpg)

The event also introduced the Dream series of seated personal eVTOL aircraft and the NA80 self-developed flight control system. Representatives from aviation organizations, universities, insurance companies, and CoolFly’s domestic and international dealer network gathered to see CoolFly’s latest personal aircraft.

Held under the theme “This Time, It Really Flies,” the launch was more than a product presentation. It brought together aircraft technology, academic research, insurance support, global distribution in a single event.

![The scene of CoolFly Launches](/assets/img/news/coolfly-launches-urban-personal-aircraft-bringing-personal-flight-2.jpg)

**Urban: A New Standing-Position Personal Aircraft**

Urban was the central focus of the launch.

Developed as a new form of personal eVTOL aircraft, Urban uses a standing-position configuration designed to make personal flight more compact and intuitive. CoolFly describes Urban as the world’s first standing-position personal aircraft.

The aircraft is fully electric and uses an enclosed-propeller propulsion system. It is equipped with CoolFly’s self-developed dual-redundant flight control architecture and triple-redundant inertial measurement units.

Urban represents CoolFly’s vision for the future of personal flight: aircraft that are lighter, more compact, easier to operate, and more practical for individual users.

While its initial applications are primarily recreational, the standing-position configuration also reflects CoolFly’s longer-term exploration of lightweight personal mobility.

![CoolFly standing aircraft URBAN](/assets/img/news/coolfly-launches-urban-personal-aircraft-bringing-personal-flight-3.jpg)

**Dream ST and Dream Pro Address Different Flight Needs**

Alongside Urban, CoolFly presented the Dream series of seated personal aircraft.

Dream ST is built around lightweight construction and features an aerospace-grade titanium alloy cage frame. Its compact seated configuration is designed to make personal electric flight more approachable and practical.

Dream Pro is designed for users seeking greater payload capacity and longer flight endurance. In manned flight testing with a 70 kg pilot, the aircraft achieved a flight time of 40 minutes and supports a maximum payload of 120 kg. It also incorporates a more advanced intelligent flight platform, including millimeter-wave obstacle-detection radar, route planning, and intelligent obstacle avoidance.

For additional protection, Dream Pro comes standard with a whole-aircraft parachute system and impact-mitigation airbags.

![CoolFly steaded aircraft DREAM PRO](/assets/img/news/coolfly-launches-urban-personal-aircraft-bringing-personal-flight-4.jpg)

**NA80: CoolFly’s Self-Developed Flight Control System**

CoolFly also officially introduced NA80, the company’s self-developed flight control system.

NA80 uses a dual-redundant flight control architecture. Each flight control unit integrates three inertial measurement units, enabling the system to continuously monitor aircraft motion and support flight control across CoolFly’s product platforms.

Rather than relying primarily on off-the-shelf core systems, CoolFly takes a full-stack approach to aircraft development. The company develops and integrates its own aircraft structures, propulsion systems, flight control technologies, and intelligent flight capabilities.

This allows CoolFly to develop its aircraft as integrated systems rather than assembling them from separate off-the-shelf components.

![CoolFly flight control System](/assets/img/news/coolfly-launches-urban-personal-aircraft-bringing-personal-flight-5.webp)

**Building a Personal Aircraft Research Platform with NUAA**

Following the product launch, CoolFly signed a cooperation agreement with Nanjing University of Aeronautics and Astronautics.

The two parties will jointly establish a personal aircraft research platform and collaborate in areas including flight control algorithms, aerodynamics, and advanced materials.

The partnership will connect academic research with real-world engineering and product development needs, supporting CoolFly’s future aircraft development, technical validation, and product iteration.

![CoolFly Building a Personal Aircraft Research Platform with NUAA](/assets/img/news/coolfly-launches-urban-personal-aircraft-bringing-personal-flight-6.jpg)

**Insurance Support for Urban and Dream**

CoolFly also signed strategic cooperation agreements with PICC Property and Casualty and China Pacific Property Insurance.

Insurance policies for Urban and Dream have already been issued, including a comprehensive insurance package and overseas product liability coverage.

With insurance coverage integrated into its product delivery and international operations, CoolFly is strengthening protection for both the company and its customers as it expands globally.

![Insurance Support for Urban and Dream](/assets/img/news/coolfly-launches-urban-personal-aircraft-bringing-personal-flight-7.jpg)

**Expanding CoolFly’s Global Dealer Network**

During the launch, two domestic dealers and two international dealers signed cooperation agreements with CoolFly.

The agreements mark another step in CoolFly’s global channel development. The company is expanding its domestic and international dealer networks in parallel, while building localized capabilities in product delivery, customer support, training, and after-sales service.

CoolFly’s dealer-led business model allows local partners to support customers throughout the ownership experience, from initial product education and purchase to delivery and ongoing service.

![Expanding CoolFly’s Global Dealer Network](/assets/img/news/coolfly-launches-urban-personal-aircraft-bringing-personal-flight-8.jpg)

**About CoolFly**

![CoolFly aircrtaft](/assets/img/news/coolfly-launches-urban-personal-aircraft-bringing-personal-flight-9.jpg)

CoolFly is a personal electric aircraft company developing standing-position and seated eVTOL platforms for individual flight.

Its product portfolio includes the Urban standing-position personal aircraft, the Dream ST and Dream Pro seated personal aircraft, and the NA80 flight control system,developed in-house.

With capabilities spanning aircraft design, electric propulsion, flight control, and intelligent flight technologies, CoolFly is building a full-stack personal aircraft platform and a global dealer network to support product delivery, customer training, and localized service.
