CoolFly is making a new world where you do not need a road to get to work. Most people look at the sky and see big planes or helicopters. But there is a special space just above the trees that we usually ignore. This is the 0-30 meter zone. CoolFly calls this the “golden vacuum layer.”

![Cinematic wide shot of a CoolFly Urban-2 gliding peacefully at 10 meters height over a lush green city park.](/assets/img/news/coolfly-living-life-at-ten-meters-above-the-ground-1.webp)

It is a place with no traffic, no red lights, and no stress. If you use a personal vtol aircraft, this space becomes your new highway. You might ask, “[what is vtol aircraft technology](https://coolflyaircraft.com/what-is-evtol-aircraft-stunning-coolfly-secrets/) exactly?” It stands for Vertical Take-Off and Landing. These machines lift you up like an elevator and then move forward. Because they are electric, they are quiet and clean. This is the future of how we will move in our cities and towns.

## **How do coolfly electric vtol aircraft work in your backyard?**

You probably want to know, “how do electric vtol aircraft work” when you are at home. These machines use many small motors instead of one loud engine. For example, the CoolFly Dream-2 has eight propellers. [This design is very safe.](https://coolflyaircraft.com/coolfly7-incredible-secrets-to-the-best-ultimate-evtol-safe/) If one motor stops, the others keep you in the air. The power comes from a high-tech battery. You do not need a special airport fuel station. You can use the same charging pile that you use for an electric car.

![Close-up of a pilot leaning forward on a CoolFly Urban-2 to steer.](/assets/img/news/coolfly-living-life-at-ten-meters-above-the-ground-2.webp)

The smartest part of the craft is the flight controller. CoolFly uses a brain called the NA80. This computer checks the balance of the craft thousands of times every second. On the Urban-2 model, you do not even need a steering wheel. You just lean your body. If you want to go forward, you lean forward. If you want to turn, you lean to the side. It feels very natural, like you are skiing in the air. [Because it is an ultralight craft, it fits under rules like FAA Part 103. In many places, this means you can fly without a complex pilot’s license.](https://aerospaceglobalnews.com/)

A trip that takes 40 minutes in a car might only take 10 minutes in the air.

You have more time for breakfast and more time with your family in the evening.

## **A hangar that fits in your garage**

One big problem with old-fashioned planes is their size. You cannot park a Cessna in your driveway. But electric vtol aircraft are different. CoolFly uses T800 carbon fiber to make the body. This material is very strong but also very light. Because of this, the craft can have a folding design.

![A modern, clean home garage. A CoolFly Urban-2 is folded neatly (2.2m long) and parked in a standard parking space next to a white electric SUV.](/assets/img/news/coolfly-living-life-at-ten-meters-above-the-ground-4.webp)

The Urban-2 can fold its arms until it is only 2.2 meters long. This is small enough to fit into a standard garage or a private parking space. You can keep it right next to your car. When you want to go on a weekend trip, you can even put it on a small trailer. You can drive to a lake or a mountain and then take off from there. You are no longer tied to an airport. Your home becomes your own private flight center.

## **Charging your coolfly like a smartphone**

We are all used to charging our phones and our cars. Charging a vtol aircraft is now just as simple. CoolFly machines use standard electric vehicle charging ports. You can plug your craft into a 40kWh power supply at home.

![A CoolFly aircraft plugged into a standard white EV charging station at a sunny lakeside resort. The charging cable is connected to a port on the side of the carbon fiber body. A smartphone nearby shows a "100% Charged" notification.](/assets/img/news/coolfly-living-life-at-ten-meters-above-the-ground-5.webp)

This is a professional answer to the “fuel” problem. You do not need to transport dangerous gasoline. The electricity is cheap and easy to find. If you go to a resort or a hotel with EV chargers, you can “fill up” while you eat lunch. This makes long-distance travel much easier. You can plan your trips based on where the charging piles are. This is how the future of green travel will actually look.

## **Helping hands from the sky**

Life at 10 meters is not just for fun or commuting. It is also for helping people. In a big city, accidents happen on the road all the time. Sometimes, an ambulance cannot reach a person because of a traffic jam. A doctor on a CoolFly aircraft can fly over the traffic and arrive in minutes.

![A CoolFly standing eVTOL used by a rescuer in a high-rise urban environment. The aircraft is hovering near a balcony of a tall glass skyscraper.](/assets/img/news/coolfly-living-life-at-ten-meters-above-the-ground-6.webp)

These machines are also perfect for high-rise buildings. If there is a fire on the 20th floor, it is hard for firefighters to reach the windows from the outside. A standing eVTOL allows a rescuer to fly directly to a specific window. They can see inside and help people out. This is a “low-altitude” solution to a high-altitude problem. It makes our cities much safer places to live.

## **Safety is the foundation**

You might worry about falling, but electric vtol aircraft are built with many layers of safety. CoolFly uses a system called “triple protection.” This means there is a parachute, safety airbags, and a smart computer that prevents mistakes.

![A technical 3D visualization of the "Triple Protection" system. A CoolFly aircraft shown with a deployed parachute, inflated landing airbags, and the NA80 flight controller glowing.](/assets/img/news/coolfly-living-life-at-ten-meters-above-the-ground-9.webp)

If the battery gets too low, the craft will automatically calculate the best way to land. If you try to turn too sharply, the NA80 brain will gently correct you. It is like having a professional co-pilot with you at all times. This technology makes flight accessible to people who are not professional pilots. It turns a complex machine into a simple tool for daily life.
