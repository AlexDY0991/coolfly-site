Recently, Mr. Ao Yuxin, Deputy Party Secretary and District Mayor of Hangzhou’s Gongshu District, led a delegation from the District Government Office, Bureau of Commerce, and other related departments to visit CoolFly (Zhejiang) Aircraft Technology Co., Ltd. for an inspection and research visit. This event aims to gain a thorough understanding of the development status of enterprises, test the progress of low-altitude economic development, and promote the implementation of relevant policies. Through on-site investigation and detailed communication, the inspection team highly affirmed Coolfly’s outstanding performance and future development potential in the low-altitude economy field. It proposed to strengthen the government’s support for Coolfly further.

![](/assets/img/news/deputy-party-secretary-and-district-mayor-of-gongshu-district-mr-ao-1.jpg)

The delegation visited the product exhibition hall of Coolfly and learned about the company’s newly developed “Near-Ground Low-altitude aircraft”. Accompanied by the CEO Li Wei, the inspection team gained an in-depth understanding of the research and development background and technical performance of the product. Li Wei focused on introducing the core technological breakthrough— AI-enabled gravity sensing technology, which has achieved full-process seamless control and significantly enhanced the stability and safety of the aircraft. The inspection team showed great interest in these innovative technologies and highly praised their application prospects in the low-altitude economy.

![](/assets/img/news/deputy-party-secretary-and-district-mayor-of-gongshu-district-mr-ao-2.jpg)

During the visit, Mr. Ao Yuxin personally stepped onto the near-ground aircraft of Coolfly Company and had a hands-on experience. District Chief Ao said that the process of personally experiencing the aircraft made him feel the safety and stability of the aircraft more directly, just like the corporate philosophy that Coolfly Company has always adhered to: “Safe travel, intelligent flight, convenient travel.”

![](/assets/img/news/deputy-party-secretary-and-district-mayor-of-gongshu-district-mr-ao-3.jpg)

During the meeting, Li Wei shared Coolfly’s enterprise expansion strategy and global layout, stating that Coolfly’s near-ground aircraft has completed over 100 flight tests, laying the foundation for technological optimization and market promotion. As an innovative representative in the low-altitude economy field, Li Wei, with his profound background in aviation technology and rich experience in the research and development of intelligent systems, has transformed Coolfly from a start-up team into an industry newcomer with both strong R&D capabilities and commercial potential.

He stated that in the future, Coolfly will continue to focus on the low-altitude economy sector, adhere to technological innovation and an international perspective, promote the application of aircraft in diverse scenarios such as urban air travel, emergency rescue, leisure and entertainment, and outdoor exploration, and continuously lead the development of China’s low-altitude aircraft industry.

![](/assets/img/news/deputy-party-secretary-and-district-mayor-of-gongshu-district-mr-ao-4.jpg)

Subsequently, the two sides exchanged views on the development trends of the low-altitude economy and industry policies. District Chief Ao emphasized that the low-altitude economy is an important force driving innovation in urban transportation, logistics, and public services. The government will continue to introduce policies, especially in coordination with relevant departments, to strongly support the development of Coolfly in terms of exhibition resources, flight airspaces, and land for office use, and looks forward to Coolfly growing into an industry benchmark.

He stated that in the future, Coolfly will continue to focus on the low-altitude economy sector, adhere to technological innovation and an international perspective, promote the application of aircraft in diverse scenarios such as urban air travel, emergency rescue, leisure and entertainment, and outdoor exploration, and continuously lead the development of China’s low-altitude aircraft industry.

![](/assets/img/news/deputy-party-secretary-and-district-mayor-of-gongshu-district-mr-ao-5.jpg)

CEO Li Wei expressed his sincere gratitude to the leaders of Gongshu District for their care and support, especially in terms of policy guidance in the fields of low-altitude economy and high-tech. This inspection is not only an examination of the development status of CoolFly, but also provides new support and direction for the company’s future development.

He stated that in the future, Coolfly will continue to focus on the low-altitude economy sector, adhere to technological innovation and an international perspective, promote the application of aircraft in diverse scenarios such as urban air travel, emergency rescue, leisure and entertainment, and outdoor exploration, and continuously lead the development of China’s low-altitude aircraft industry.

![](/assets/img/news/deputy-party-secretary-and-district-mayor-of-gongshu-district-mr-ao-6.jpg)

In the future, Coolfly will closely cooperate with the government of Gongshu District to jointly explore new opportunities in the low-altitude economy and promote the development of future urban air travel and international application scenarios.
