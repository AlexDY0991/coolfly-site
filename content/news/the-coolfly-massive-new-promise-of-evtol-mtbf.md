## **Understanding Coolfly MTBF in Aviation**

  
We must talk about the math behind the magic. Reliability engineering uses a specific metric. Professionals call this MTBF. The letters stand for Mean Time Between Failures. This number tells us how long a machine operates before a problem happens. A high MTBF means high safety. A flying machine cannot break down in the sky. We cannot park an aircraft on a cloud. Every part must work perfectly.
