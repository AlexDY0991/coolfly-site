The desert sun over Abu Dhabi is intense, but the heat inside the ADNEC (Abu Dhabi National Exhibition Centre) is driven by pure innovation. As we stand at the CoolFly booth during UMEX 2026, our team isn’t just showcasing a product; we are witnessing a paradigm shift in human mobility.

![](/assets/img/news/over-the-joystick-dispatches-from-the-coolfly-team-at-umex-2026-1.webp)

From the moment the exhibition doors opened, our booth has been a global crossroads. We’ve welcomed high-ranking military officials from the Gulf, tech visionaries from Europe, and emergency response experts from across Asia. Their question is almost always the same: “Where is the cockpit? Where are the joysticks?”

Our answer, which never fails to spark a look of pure wonder, is simple: “You are the pilot. Your body is the joystick.”

## **The Moment of Intuition**

We spent most of the morning guiding a senior aviation consultant onto the platform of our CoolFly Urban. Like most experts, he arrived with a healthy dose of skepticism. He was looking for buttons, throttles, and complex interfaces.

![](/assets/img/news/over-the-joystick-dispatches-from-the-coolfly-team-at-umex-2026-2.jpg)

“Just stand naturally,” We told him. “Lean where you want to go.”

As he shifted his weight slightly forward, our NA80 Flight Control System—the digital brain we spent years perfecting—interpreted his intent in milliseconds. The four-shrouded fans hummed into life with a smooth, sophisticated tone. He didn’t just move; he glided. Seeing that “Aha!” moment on a customer’s face—the realization that flight has finally become as intuitive as walking—is why we do what we do.

![](/assets/img/news/over-the-joystick-dispatches-from-the-coolfly-team-at-umex-2026-3.jpg)

## **Why CoolFly? Why Standing?**

Visitors often ask us why we chose a standing posture. As we explain to them, standing isn’t just a design choice; it’s a functional revolution.

l **Unmatched Visibility:** In rescue or commuting, a standing pilot has a 360-degree field of view that no cockpit can match.

![](/assets/img/news/over-the-joystick-dispatches-from-the-coolfly-team-at-umex-2026-4.jpg)

l **The 0-30 Meter “Golden Vacuum”:** While others aim for the clouds, we own the “Golden Vacuum Layer.” This 30-meter altitude is where real-world problems—like urban congestion and high-rise fires—are solved.

l **Safety Through Intelligence:** We show our guests the internals of our NA80 system. With six-redundant IMUs and military-grade chips, we’ve built a system where the software compensates for the environment, allowing the human to focus purely on the destination.

## **Leading the Low-Altitude Revolution**

As staff members of CoolFly (Zhejiang), we are proud to share our heritage. Backed by top-tier academicians and aerospace veterans, we’ve moved beyond being a “drone company.” We are the architects of Near-Ground Personal Mobility.

Abu Dhabi has been the perfect stage for us. The UAE’s vision for a “Smart City” aligns perfectly with our mission: to make flight a basic human right, not a specialized skill.

UMEX 2026 isn’t over yet, but one thing is certain: the future of flight isn’t sitting down. It’s standing up, leaning forward, and embracing the sky with nothing but your instinct.
