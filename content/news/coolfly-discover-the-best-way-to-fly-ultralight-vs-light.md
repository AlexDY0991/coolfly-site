## **The Basic Rules: Understanding the Coolfly Divide**

The biggest difference between these two types of aircraft is not how they look. It is about the law. Most countries follow rules made by the Federal Aviation Administration (FAA). In the United States, the rule is called Part 103. This rule defines what an ultralight vehicle is.

An ultralight aircraft is built for one person. It is used only for fun or sport. The most important part is the weight. If it has a motor, it must weigh less than 254 pounds. This is about 115 kilograms. It also has a speed limit. It cannot go faster than 55 knots, which is 102 kilometers per hour.

![Close-up technical shot of a single-occupant coolfly ultralight aircraft](/assets/img/news/coolfly-discover-the-best-way-to-fly-ultralight-vs-light-2.webp)

A light aircraft, such as a Light Sport Aircraft (LSA), is different. These planes can carry more people. They are much heavier. Because they are bigger and faster, the government requires more from the owner. You must have a pilot license to fly one. [The aircraft must also have a special safety paper called an airworthiness certificate.](http://aerospaceglobalnews.com)

## **Why Weight and Speed Matter**

You might wonder why the weight limit is so low for ultralights. It is about physics and safety. The government uses a logic called “trading physics for freedom.” If a flying machine is very light and slow, it carries very little energy. If something goes wrong, it cannot cause a lot of damage to people on the ground.

As the FAA states in the Part 103 rules, these vehicles are “limited to a single occupant” and are intended for “recreational or aeronautical sport purposes only” (FAA Part 103.1). By following these tight limits, owners do not need to register the plane. They do not need a medical exam. Most importantly, they do not need to take a flying test.

## **How Do Coolfly Electric VTOL Aircraft Work?**

To understand why these machines are better than old planes, we must ask: how do electric VTOL aircraft work?

Old planes need a long runway to catch the wind and lift off. VTOL aircraft do not need runways. They use multiple small rotors to push air straight down. This allows them to lift off from a backyard or a parking space. CoolFly uses a “four-axis, eight-propeller” design. This means if one motor stops, the others keep you safe.

![A futuristic coolfly eVTOL aircraft parked next to a modern electric vehicle charging station](/assets/img/news/coolfly-discover-the-best-way-to-fly-ultralight-vs-light-4.webp)

The “electric” part is also key. Electric VTOL aircraft use batteries instead of gas. This makes them quiet. It also makes them much easier to build. You do not need a complex engine with hundreds of moving parts. You only need a battery, a controller, and motors. Our CoolFly products even use the same charging plugs as electric cars. You can charge your flying machine at the same station where you charge your Tesla.

## **Comparing Coolfly Ultralight and Light VTOLs**

When we look at the future of city travel, we see both types of vtol aircraft working together. However, they solve different problems.

Safety Requirements: Light aircraft need “Type Certification.” This takes 2 to 5 years and costs millions of dollars. Ultralight aircraft like the CoolFly Dream-2 are “exempt.” This means they can reach customers much faster.

Training: To fly a light aircraft, you need at least 20 to 40 hours of training with a teacher. For a CoolFly Urban-2, you use our “Gravity-Sensing” tech. You just lean your body to move. It is as simple as walking.

Cost: Because light aircraft need so many permits and more power, they are very expensive. Ultralight eVTOLs are designed to be “consumer products.” They are meant to be affordable for personal fun.

I have observed that the industry is moving toward “Software Defined Flight.” In my research, I see that the hardware is becoming simpler, but the “brains” are getting smarter. For example, CoolFly uses a 6-redundancy IMU system. This is a fancy way of saying the flight computer checks itself six times every second to keep you level. Even if the pilot makes a mistake, the computer prevents the machine from flipping over.

Why does CoolFly stay so low? Most people think the future of flight is high in the clouds. We disagree. The space between 0 and 30 meters is a “Golden Vacuum.”

At 120 meters, you have helicopters and big planes. The rules are very strict. Between 30 and 120 meters, there are thousands of delivery drones and camera drones. It is a crowded space. But under 30 meters, the air is empty. This is where personal travel can happen safely. It avoids the complex traffic of the higher sky. It stays close to the user’s needs.
