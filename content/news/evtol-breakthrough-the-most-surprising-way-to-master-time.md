### **Defining the New Standard: What is eVTOL Aircraft?**

To understand the impact of this vehicle on your schedule, we must first answer the question: [what is evtol aircraft](https://coolflyaircraft.com/what-is-evtol-aircraft-stunning-coolfly-secrets/) and how does it optimize travel? An evtol is an Electric Vertical Take-off and Landing vehicle. These vtol aircraft use electricity to power multiple rotors, which allows them to lift off from very small spaces. Because the Coolfly is an electric vtol aircraft, it does not need a long runway or a massive hangar. Instead, it is designed to fit into the existing urban landscape.

![A technical studio shot of the Coolfly eVTOL in its folded state, dimensions 2200 × 926 × 1620 mm clearly visible in its compact form.](/assets/img/news/evtol-breakthrough-the-most-surprising-way-to-master-time-2.webp)

The physical specifications of the Coolfly are particularly impressive because they solve the “storage problem” that plagues larger aircraft. When the vehicle is folded, its dimensions are just 2200 × 926 × 1620 mm. This compact footprint means you can store the vehicle in a standard parking space or even on a large balcony. Since the vehicle is so small, you do not waste time traveling to a distant airport to start your journey. You can start your flight closer to your home or office. Therefore, the total “door-to-door” travel time is significantly lower than any other form of private aviation.

The most critical metric for any commuter is speed. During extensive field trials, the Coolfly test group confirmed that the standing flight vehicle reaches a maximum speed of 70 km/h. While 70 km/h might seem comparable to a car on a highway, the reality of an eVTOL vs ground transport travel time comparison is much more dramatic. A car must follow winding roads, wait at red lights, and navigate through intersections. Because of these obstacles, the “average” speed of a car in a city like London or New York often drops below 15 km/h during peak hours.
