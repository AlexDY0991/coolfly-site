In the golden autumn season, the global attention of the low-altitude economy sector converged on London, UK – the three-day UK AirTaxi World Congress grandly opened. As a premier global event for electric aviation and low-altitude transportation, this exhibition brought together over 200 leading companies, research institutions, and industry experts from more than 30 countries to discuss the development trends, technological breakthroughs, and commercialization paths of the low-altitude economy. On this star-studded international stage, CoolFly (Zhejiang) Aircraft Technology Co., Ltd., from China, stood out from numerous exhibitors with its forward-looking product portfolio, robust technological strength, and clear global strategy, becoming an undisputed “rising star” at the exhibition and showcasing the powerful capabilities of China’s low-altitude flight technology to the world.

**Multidimensional Strength as the Foundation**  
Top-Tier Technology Empowers: Building a Distinctive Product Matrix and Consolidating Core Competitive Barriers  
Since its inception, CoolFly has consistently regarded technological innovation as the core driving force for its development. Relying on an internationally leading R&D system and technological reserves, it has successfully broken through several key industry technologies, creating a series of highly competitive and influential aircraft products, providing solid support for the diversified implementation of low-altitude flight scenarios.

At the core product level, CoolFly’s product matrix covers multiple key areas such as manned flight and core control systems: Near-ground manned aircraft, with their safe and stable performance and flexible adaptability, can efficiently meet the diverse needs of scenarios such as urban low-altitude commuting, rapid emergency rescue response, and precise monitoring for mountain patrols; Seated manned flying go-karts, designed with “compact and convenient” as their core, integrate fun experiences and intelligent flight technology, opening up a new track in the low-altitude entertainment experience market; The world’s first dedicated eVTOL flight control system further demonstrates CoolFly’s leading position in core technology fields. This system integrates advanced functions such as accuracy prediction and cruise management, ensuring the flight safety and control stability of eVTOL aircraft from the underlying logic, filling a gap in related technology applications within the industry. To ensure leading performance and safety standards, CoolFly employs a series of advanced technologies, including absolute safety technology, battery rigidity reinforcement technology, and fast-charging technology compatible with charging stations. Absolute safety technology constructs a multi-dimensional safety protection system from flight attitude control and emergency fault handling, minimizing flight risks. Battery rigidity technology is specifically optimized for the core power components of the aircraft, ensuring stable battery operation in complex environments such as low temperatures and turbulence, extending flight time, and providing highly accurate range prediction. The groundbreaking fast-charging technology compatible with charging stations can replenish 20%-85% of the battery within 15 minutes, significantly reducing aircraft recharging time and effectively addressing the pain point of “slow recharging” in low-altitude flight, providing efficient support for high-frequency flight operations and daily use. With these technological advantages, CoolFly products have formed three core characteristics: “safety and stability, AI autonomous flight, and compact and convenient,” becoming a benchmark for technology and quality in the low-altitude flight field.
