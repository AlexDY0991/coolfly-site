On November 17, 2025, at the Dubai World Centre, the desert winds carried the passion of technology as the 16th Dubai Airshow opened amidst great anticipation. Coolfly (Zhejiang) Aircraft Technology Co., Ltd. arrived on the sands with its first-generation near-ground manned aircraft, presenting a poetic picture of “future mobility” to the Middle East at booth 94 of AAM PAVILION – this was not only the Middle East debut of Chinese low-altitude technology, but also a gentle dialogue between “humans and flight.”

![](/assets/img/news/made-in-china-arrives-on-the-sands-coolfly-makes-its-dubai-airshow-1.jpg)

01\. Glimpses: A Stunning Aerial Aesthetics at a Glance

Around 10:00 AM, the light and shadow of the exhibition hall fell on the streamlined fuselage of the first-generation near-ground manned aircraft, instantly outlining its technological and elegant silhouette. Without a complicated steering wheel or heavy pedals, its unique “standing control” design allows “flight” to return to its lightest state: the pilot only needs to slightly adjust their center of gravity to control the direction as if walking on the wind. Behind this lightness lies rigorous adherence to general aviation safety standards—Coolfly’s self-developed NA80, the world’s first eVTOL dedicated flight controller, and aviation-grade triple-redundant IMU, weave an invisible safety net, allowing even first-timers to easily experience the comfort of “walking in the clouds.”

Among the onlookers, several visitors lingered, repeatedly examining the details of the fuselage. One murmured, “This is exactly the agile solution Dubai’s air commuter network needs, and what’s even more remarkable is how solidly it’s implemented in terms of safety.” This device, affectionately called a “real-life flying skateboard” by the audience, is Coolfly’s poetic interpretation of “safety first.” According to the on-site representative from Coolfly, this aircraft not only boasts a maximum payload of 110kg and a 30-minute flight time, capable of handling emergency rescue and sightseeing needs, but also fully complies with CCAR-23 light aircraft airworthiness standards in terms of safety. It has already completed over 2000 low-altitude test flights with a zero-accident record, and mass production is expected to become a reality in 2026.

![](/assets/img/news/made-in-china-arrives-on-the-sands-coolfly-makes-its-dubai-airshow-2.jpg)

02\. In-Depth Technical Exploration: The Wisdom of Extreme Environments Hidden in the Details

Dubai’s sandstorms and scorching sun pose a severe test to aircraft performance. Every technical detail of Coolfly’s systems reflects not only environmental adaptation but also adherence to general aviation safety principles:  
IP65 waterproof and dustproof engineering acts like a “sandstorm armor” for the aircraft, ensuring stable operation even when faced with strong desert winds carrying fine sand; the world’s first eVTOL flight control system constructs an “airborne fault-tolerant barrier” with nine layers of safety protection. Its power redundancy design follows the general aviation “single-failure safety” principle—even if a single power unit fails, the remaining system can still support a safe landing; the addition of military-grade aviation connectors and aviation-grade inertial navigation components acts like a “precision navigator,” maintaining a stable trajectory even in complex environments.

![](/assets/img/news/made-in-china-arrives-on-the-sands-coolfly-makes-its-dubai-airshow-3.jpg)

03\. Market Resonance: The Synchronization of Chinese Solutions and Middle Eastern Needs

“From the airport to Palm Jumeirah, a one-hour ground journey takes only 12 minutes by air, saving 73% of the time—but what interests us even more is that it fully complies with international general aviation safety standards.” This observation from a Middle Eastern aircraft leasing company highlights the perfect fit between Coolfly and the local market. As one of the top ten brands at the World Digital Cities Conference, Coolfly showcased not just a single product, but a comprehensive low-altitude transportation solution led by academicians of the Chinese Academy of Sciences, with general aviation safety at its core. From the core technology of the flight control system to safety training for later maintenance, everything is designed according to general aviation operation standards. For example, it provides pilot training that meets international general aviation standards, along with a real-time flight data monitoring platform, achieving a “turnkey” service while solidifying the foundation for safe operation.

This deep adaptation, prioritizing safety, perfectly aligns with the development pace of the Middle Eastern aviation industry. In recent years, local operators have consistently prioritized aviation safety standards when developing the low-altitude transportation sector. From commuting to energy bases to high-end sightseeing, the demand for manned aircraft that are both safe and compliant, as well as flexible and practical, is increasingly urgent. Coolfly’s technological approach precisely fills this market gap.

04\. Guide to the Event: A Warm Encounter with the Future of Travel  
From its initial debut at the China International Import Expo to its dazzling display at the Dubai Airshow, Coolfly has measured the globalization of China’s low-altitude technology with its 55km range. If you happen to be strolling through Dubai World Centre, consider visiting booth No.94 AAM PAVILION during the exhibition period from November 17th to 21st. Here, you can witness the technological beauty of the next generation of near-ground manned aircraft, experience the refined texture of the streamlined fuselage up close, listen to the technical team’s detailed explanation of the future of low-altitude travel, and personally feel the new imagination of flight brought to the desert skies by Chinese intelligent manufacturing.
