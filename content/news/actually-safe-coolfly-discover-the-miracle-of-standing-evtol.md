### **The New Way to Fly: What is Coolfly eVTOL Aircraft Design?**

![left side shows a bulky traditional flying car with a cockpit and seats, right side shows a sleek minimalist Coolfly standing evtol aircraft](/assets/img/news/actually-safe-coolfly-discover-the-miracle-of-standing-evtol-1.webp)

I am part of the team at Coolfly. We spend our days rethinking how humans move through the sky. Many people come to us with a basic question: [what is evtol aircraft?](https://coolflyaircraft.com/what-is-a-vtol-aircraft/) VTOL stands for Vertical Take-off and Landing. Most people imagine a small helicopter or a flying car with seats. However, our machine is different. We built a standing-posture vehicle. People often ask me why we did not just put a chair in it. They want to know the real difference between standing and sitting in the air. This choice was not just about looking cool. It was a calculated move to solve the biggest problems in the future of flight.

### **The Weight Battle: Why Getting Rid of the Seat Matters**

When we started building the Coolfly electric vtol aircraft, weight was our biggest enemy. In the world of aviation, every gram counts. Seated flying vehicles are heavy. They need a strong frame to hold a chair and a cabin to protect the pilot. They also need heavy flight controls like joysticks and pedals. All these parts add up quickly. A typical seated eVTOL can weigh hundreds of kilograms.

![A technical close-up of an ultralight electric vtol aircraft frame](/assets/img/news/actually-safe-coolfly-discover-the-miracle-of-standing-evtol-2.webp)

This weight creates a legal problem. In the United States, the FAA Part 103 rule is very important for the low-altitude economy. It allows people to fly “ultralight” vehicles without a pilot’s license. But there is a catch. The empty vehicle must weigh less than 254 pounds (about 115 kg).

By removing the seat and the cabin, we saved a massive amount of weight. Our aircraft weighs only about 110 kg. [According to news reports, “By keeping the aircraft ultralight—around 110 kg—Coolfly says the vehicle fits within FAA Part 103 ultralight rules” (Aerospace Global News).](http://aerospaceglobalnews.com) This is a miracle for the average person. It means you can fly our machine legally without spending years in flight school. Seated models simply cannot meet this weight limit.

### **Control by Instinct: How Do Coolfly Electric VTOL Aircraft Work?**

Another major difference is how you actually steer the machine. People often ask, how do electric vtol aircraft work if there is no steering wheel? In a seated aircraft, you use your hands and feet. You have to learn how to move multiple levers at the same time. This takes a lot of training. If you get scared, you might pull the wrong handle.

![A person standing on a coolfly aircraft, leaning forward to accelerate](/assets/img/news/actually-safe-coolfly-discover-the-miracle-of-standing-evtol-3.webp)

We wanted a more natural way to fly. We developed an AI gravity-sensing flight control system. This system uses the way you stand to move the craft. When you stand on the Coolfly platform, your body becomes the joystick. You lean forward to go fast,lean back to slow down,lean left or right to turn. This is much easier for the human brain to understand. We call this “bionic flight” logic. Because you are standing, your inner ear and your legs work together to keep you balanced. This is a unique advantage of the standing posture. It turns flying into a natural instinct, like walking or skiing.

### **Seeing Everything: The 360-Degree Advantage**

When you sit in a traditional flying car, your view is limited. You have a floor beneath you and pillars around the windows. You have to look through a small piece of glass. This can be difficult when you are trying to land in a tight space. In the low-altitude sky, visibility is a huge safety factor. You need to see birds, wires, and other vehicles.

![POV shot from a standing pilot on a coolfly evtol, 360-degree unobstructed view of a futuristic city with rivers and parks](/assets/img/news/actually-safe-coolfly-discover-the-miracle-of-standing-evtol-4.webp)

Standing up gives you a perfect view of the world. There are no walls or ceilings to block your eyes. You can look straight down at your feet to see exactly where you are landing. This 360-degree vision is a game-changer for safety. During our research, we found that pilots feel more confident when they can see the environment clearly. The standing posture makes the machine feel smaller and more manageable. It is like the difference between driving a closed van and riding a bicycle. You feel connected to the air around you.

### **Defeating Panic: Safety Without Levers**

We also thought deeply about human fear. When an emergency happens, people often panic. In a seated cockpit, a panicking pilot might grab a lever and pull it too hard. This is a common cause of accidents. We decided to remove the source of the problem. Our machine has no levers or buttons for steering.

![Close-up of enclosed coolfly evtol propfan propulsion system, solid protective rings](/assets/img/news/actually-safe-coolfly-discover-the-miracle-of-standing-evtol-5.webp)

Because you are standing, the AI handles the stability for you. If a sudden wind hits the craft, our redundant IMU sensors detect the tilt. The computer adjusts the motors faster than you can blink. You do not need to do anything. You just keep standing naturally. This design prevents “pilot error” during scary moments. We also used enclosed propfans to protect you. In many seated designs, the spinning blades are exposed. This is a huge danger. Our fans are hidden inside strong rings. This means you can stand on the board and feel safe. You know that the moving parts cannot touch you.

### **Coolfly Real-World Testing: Lessons from the Cold**

We don’t just guess about these differences. We test them in the toughest places on Earth. Last year, we took the Coolfly aircraft to Harbin. The temperature was minus 30 degrees Celsius. In that kind of cold, sitting still in a cockpit is very uncomfortable. Your blood moves slowly, and your reactions get dull.

![Coolfly standing evtol aircraft flying in a freezing snow-covered city (Harbin)](/assets/img/news/actually-safe-coolfly-discover-the-miracle-of-standing-evtol-6.webp)

Standing up keeps your body active. Your muscles are working to maintain balance. This keeps you warm and alert. We observed that our test riders stayed focused for longer periods than pilots in seated simulators. We also tested how the battery and motors handled the ice. Even in the freezing wind, our redundant systems kept the flight stable. This research proved that a standing vehicle is not just a toy. It is a professional tool that works in harsh conditions. It is portable enough to fit in a small van, while seated vehicles need a large trailer or a hangar.
